01What does BeT Agency Co. do?

BeT Agency Co. is a premium digital studio. We design and build websites, landing pages, custom CRMs, Sanity CMS setups, business automation and custom web apps, all crafted from scratch for brands that mean business.

02Who do you work with?

Businesses that are serious about growing. Founders and teams who see their website as part of how the business runs, not a box to tick, and who care about quality as much as we do.

03How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted after a conversation, never from a menu. We first learn your business, your audience and where you want to be, then plan the right build and share a clear quote. Our work sits at the premium end, and it is priced that way.

04How long does a project take?

It depends on what we are building. A landing page moves quickly, while CRMs and custom platforms are planned in phases. You get a clear timeline before any work begins, and you know which stage we are in throughout.

05Can I update the website myself?

Yes. With a Sanity CMS setup you can change text, images, products and pages yourself, without touching code or waiting on us.

06What happens after launch?

We hand over the keys, show your team how everything works and stay on call as you grow, whether that means new pages, new automations or a CRM to manage the leads your site brings in.

07How do we get started?

Tap Place your bet and tell us about your business. It takes about two minutes. You can also email bet.agency01@gmail.com or message us on Instagram at @betagency.co.
