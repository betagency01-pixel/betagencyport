Privacy, plainly.

Last updated: November 2026

BeT Agency Co. ("we") runs this website. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

When you send an enquiry

We save what you type in the enquiry form: your name, phone number, email, brand name, details about your business, the services you're interested in, your budget range and timeline. We use it only to reply to you and plan your project. We never sell it or share it with advertisers.

Visit statistics

To understand which parts of the site are useful, we count visits on our own servers. No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no ad networks. For every visit we record:

  • the pages and sections you looked at, and buttons you used (for example, flipping a service card)
  • approximate location (city and country), worked out by our hosting provider from your connection. We do not store your IP address
  • device type, browser, screen size and language
  • the website or campaign that sent you here, if any
  • how long the visit lasted

Cookies

If you choose Accept, we set two of our own cookies:

  • bet_consent remembers your choice (6 months).
  • bet_vid is a random id so we can tell a returning visitor from a new one (1 year). It contains nothing about who you are.

If you choose Only essentials, we only keep your choice. Your visit is still counted anonymously, with no id that links it to future visits.

How long we keep data

Enquiries are kept while we're in touch and for up to 2 years after. Visit statistics are kept for up to 2 years and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete anything we hold about you. Email bet.agency01@gmail.com and we'll reply within 7 days.
